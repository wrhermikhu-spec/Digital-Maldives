Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what information Digital Maldives ("we", "us") collects when you use this website to buy gift cards, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Information you give us

  • Name and phone number or email, so we can identify your order and contact you if there is a problem.
  • Payment slip: the screenshot of your bank transfer, so we can verify your payment.

2. Information collected automatically

When you place an order, we automatically record:

  • IP address, as seen by our server and as seen by your browser, together with your internet provider and approximate location (city, region and country) based on that IP address.
  • Device information: phone or device model, operating system, browser, screen size, language and timezone.
  • A random browser identifier stored on your device, used to link orders placed from the same browser.
  • The date and time of your order.

Website visits: when you visit our store, even without placing an order, we record your IP address (as seen by our server and by your browser), internet provider, approximate location based on IP, device information (as listed above), the page visited, the referring website, and the date, time and number of your visits. This helps us detect fraud and abuse and understand how the store is used.

3. Precise location (GPS)

We never ask you for your location. However, if you have previously allowed this website to access your location in your browser or phone settings, we record your GPS coordinates and their accuracy with your order, to help prevent fraud. You can turn this off at any time by removing location permission for this website in your browser or phone settings.

4. Identity verification

If an order looks suspicious, we may ask you to verify your identity before we deliver it. In that case we ask for a photo of your ID card and a selfie of you holding it. Our staff compare these manually with your order and payment details. They are used only to verify that order and prevent fraud. They are stored privately, seen only by authorised staff, and deleted after we have reviewed them, within 30 days at most. You may choose not to verify; in that case we will cancel the order and refund any payment we received.

Your browser also stores a list of your recent orders on your own device so you can find them under "My orders". We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Why we use your information

  • To verify payments and deliver your gift card PIN.
  • To prevent fraud, such as fake or reused payment slips, stolen accounts or repeated abuse.
  • To contact you about your order.
  • To keep records of sales and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your information or use it for marketing.

6. Who can see it

Only our authorised staff can view your order details, payment slip, ID photos (if requested), device and location information. Your data is stored with service providers who host this website and its database (Vercel and Supabase). Your browser's IP address and internet provider are looked up using an IP lookup service (ipapi.co, or ipify.org as a backup). These providers process data only to provide their service. We may share information with banks, payment providers or authorities when required by law or to investigate fraud.

7. Your order link

After you place an order you receive a private link to your order page. Anyone with this link can see your order status and, once approved, your PIN. Keep the link private.

8. How long we keep it

We keep order records, payment slips, device and location information for up to 12 months after your order for fraud prevention, disputes and accounting, unless a longer period is required by law. After that, we delete them.

9. Security

Our database is not publicly accessible, payment slips are stored privately, and our admin area is password-protected. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us below. We may need to keep some records where required for fraud prevention or legal reasons.

11. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
