1. Definitions

In this Policy, unless the context requires otherwise:

  • "Personal information" means any information that identifies, relates to, describes, or is reasonably capable of being associated with an identified or identifiable individual.
  • "Processing" means any operation performed on personal information, including collection, recording, organisation, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, combination, restriction, erasure or destruction.
  • "Order" means a request submitted through the Service to purchase one or more digital gift card codes.
  • "PIN" or "Code" means a digital gift card code, voucher, redemption code or similar credential delivered through the Service.
  • "Payment Slip" means any image, screenshot, receipt or other record of a bank transfer that you upload in connection with an Order.
  • "Visitor" means any person who accesses any page of the Service, whether or not that person places an Order.
  • "Buyer" means any person who submits an Order.
  • "Device" means any computer, phone, tablet or other equipment used to access the Service.

5. Location information

5.1 Approximate location

As described in Section 4, we derive an approximate location from your IP address for every Visitor and Buyer.

5.2 Precise location (GPS)

Precise location (such as GPS coordinates) is only available to us where your browser permits it. We collect precise location in the following circumstances only:

  1. Where you have previously granted this website location permission in your browser or device settings. In that case, when you open checkout, we read your current coordinates and their accuracy without displaying any additional prompt, and store them with your Order. We do not request location permission at checkout.
  2. Where you choose to share your location during identity verification, by pressing the "Share my location" button. In that case, your browser will ask for your permission and we only receive your location if you allow it. Sharing location at this step is optional. We may take into account whether location was shared when assessing a verification request.

You can withdraw location permission for this website at any time through your browser or device settings. Withdrawing permission does not affect information collected before withdrawal.

6. Identity verification information

Where an Order presents indicators of potential fraud, error or misuse, we may place the Order on hold and request that you verify your identity. Verification consists of uploading (a) a photograph of the front of a government-issued identity document, and (b) a photograph of yourself holding that document, and may optionally include sharing your location as described in Section 5.2.

Verification materials are reviewed manually by our authorised staff, who compare the face, name and other details with the Order and Payment Slip. We do not use automated facial recognition or biometric matching. Verification materials are used solely to verify the relevant Order and to prevent and investigate fraud. They are stored in a private storage area that is not publicly accessible, and are deleted after the verification decision is made, and in any event no later than thirty (30) days after the decision, unless we are required to retain them by law or in connection with an ongoing investigation or dispute.

You are not obliged to complete verification. If you decline or do not complete verification, we may cancel the Order and refund any payment received in accordance with our Terms of Use.

6.1 Instant delivery

If you choose Instant delivery at checkout, you upload a photograph of your identity document together with your Payment Slip. These images are sent to our text recognition (OCR) service provider (see Section 12), which converts the text in the images into plain text. Our system then reads specific details from that text: the sender name, recipient name and account, amount, currency, date and time and transaction reference from the Payment Slip, and the name, document number and expiry date from the identity document. Our system then automatically compares these details with your Order, including whether the sender name matches the name on your identity document, whether the transfer was made within 30 minutes of the time you opened checkout, whether the amount and recipient account are correct, and whether the transaction reference has been used before. We record the time you opened checkout for this purpose. The extracted details and the result of each comparison are stored with your Order. Identity documents uploaded for Instant delivery are retained and deleted in the same way as other verification materials described above.

7. Information stored on your device

The Service uses your browser's local storage to keep (a) a randomly generated browser identifier, used to recognise repeat visits and to link Orders placed from the same browser, and (b) a list of your recent Orders, so that you can find them under "My orders". Our admin area also uses session storage to keep staff signed in. We do not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking pixels or cross-site tracking technologies. You can clear local storage at any time through your browser settings; doing so will remove the identifier and your saved order list from that browser.

8. How we use your information

We process personal information for the following purposes:

  1. Providing the Service: displaying the store, processing Orders, verifying payments and delivering PINs.
  2. Payment verification: matching Payment Slips against funds received.
  3. Fraud prevention and security: detecting, investigating and preventing fraudulent, unauthorised, abusive or illegal activity, including the use of fake, edited or reused Payment Slips, stolen bank accounts, multiple identities or automated access.
  4. Identity verification: where requested under Section 6.
  5. Customer support: responding to enquiries, complaints, refund requests and disputes.
  6. Operations and improvement: understanding how the Service is used, measuring traffic and diagnosing technical problems.
  7. Legal and compliance: keeping business and accounting records, complying with legal obligations, responding to lawful requests, and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for advertising or marketing, and we do not send marketing messages.

9. Legal basis for processing

To the extent applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on: (a) the performance of a contract with you, or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, for processing Orders; (b) our legitimate interests in operating a secure Service, preventing fraud and protecting our business, Buyers and third parties, for the collection of technical, network and device information; (c) your consent, for precise location collected under Section 5.2 and, where required, for identity verification materials; and (d) compliance with legal obligations, for record keeping and responses to lawful requests.

10. Fraud prevention and automated signals

Our admin tools display indicators that may help our staff identify potentially fraudulent activity, for example when the same IP address is used by multiple Buyers, when the IP address observed by our server differs from the IP address observed by your browser, or when a Buyer previously had an Order rejected. These indicators are reviewed by a person.

Where you choose Instant delivery, a PIN may be released automatically if every automated check described in Section 6.1 is passed. The automated system can only approve an Order; it never rejects an Order. If any check is not passed, or the automated check cannot be completed, your Order is referred to our staff for manual review in the ordinary way. You can always choose Standard delivery, which does not involve automated document reading.

12. Service providers and international transfers

We use the following categories of service providers, which may process personal information on our behalf and may store it in countries other than the Maldives:

  • Website hosting and serverless functions: Vercel Inc., which also provides the approximate IP-based location of requests.
  • Database and file storage: Supabase Inc., which stores Orders, Payment Slips, verification materials and visit records.
  • Text recognition (Instant delivery only): OCR.space, operated by a9t9 software GmbH, which converts the Payment Slip and identity document images you upload for Instant delivery into text so that we can read the details described in Section 6.1. OCR.space states that uploaded files and extracted text are deleted immediately after processing.
  • IP lookup: ipapi.co and, as a fallback, ipify.org, which your browser contacts directly to determine your public IP address and network operator.
  • Fonts: Google Fonts, which your browser contacts to load typefaces and which may receive your IP address.

Where personal information is transferred outside the Maldives, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is protected in a manner consistent with this Policy.
